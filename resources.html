NAMI programs to know about

NAMI (National Alliance on Mental Illness) offers peer-led programs and help finding local support. These are a few entry points; details vary by region.

  • NAMI HelpLine — information, resources, and support for individuals and families.
  • Family-to-Family — education for family members of someone living with mental illness.
  • Peer-led groups — structured spaces for people with lived experience and for loved ones.

Visit nami.org for current program information in your area.
